About

Let's talk about data

Misaligned, incomplete, spread across five agencies. That's usually where the interesting answer is hiding.

I'm a senior at Northeastern studying data science and mathematics, and a data analyst in the Structural Futures Lab under Prof. Demi Fang. Over the past year I've matched two million building footprints to attribute data, fit survival models to work out why some buildings come down and others stand, and built the dashboards that make those answers legible to people who don't write code.

Before that I spent six months at Beth Israel Deaconess doing quantitative neuroimaging — susceptibility mapping, oxygen extraction fraction, cerebral microbleed detection — on an NIH-funded study. Different domain, same shape of problem: noisy measurements, a real question underneath, and a pipeline standing between the two.

What I care about is the whole path rather than one slice of it: ingest, clean, model, evaluate honestly, then ship an interface!

Away from the screen I produce music and play clarinet, so the staff on the home page is a real one — five lines, real chord voicings, and it actually plays. No audio files anywhere; that's just the browser making noise. Switch it to 4-3-3 and the same eleven dots stop being chords and start being a football team, which tells you most of what you need to know about how my brain files things. I watch a great deal more soccer than I play, and the team is Manchester United — for better or worse.

Five lines, a back four, a floor plan, a spreadsheet — give me a grid and I'll put something on it. It's the same itch that gets me cleaning up a dataset nobody asked me to touch, and the same one that has me redrawing the same chart four times before I'll show it to anyone.

Massachusetts Building Analysis Dataset & Dashboard

Structural Futures Lab · 2025 – 2026

  • Fused 2M+ building polygons with point-level attribute data using multi-stage spatial joins and nearest-neighbor matching across the state.
  • Merged five national and city datasets — USA Structures, NSI, Web Soil Survey, MassGIS — into one GeoPackage with occupancy conflict detection, soil property linkage, and material/foundation classification.
  • Wrote the Python preprocessor: cleaning, K-Means clustering, soil composition analysis, and chunked JSON export sized for the browser.
  • Shipped 20+ Plotly and D3 views covering building age, occupancy, height, floor area, material, and data quality.
Scale
2,091,488 polygons · 5 source datasets
Role
Data engineering, modeling, front end — end to end
Stack
Python · GeoPandas · Pandas · scikit-learn · Plotly.js · D3.js
Output
Unified GeoPackage + 20-view dashboard

Boston Building Lifespan & Survival Analysis

Structural Futures Lab · 2025 – 2026

  • Spatially joined 69K+ Boston building records with zoning boundaries, then computed lifespan, material and foundation distributions, and floor area statistics for demolished versus standing stock.
  • Fit six survival models — Kaplan-Meier, Nelson-Aalen, Cox PH, Weibull AFT, Random Survival Forest, DeepSurv — to estimate demolition probability.
  • Found deep models overfitting at small event counts while traditional parametric models held up.
  • Built a single-page dashboard with 20+ Chart.js and Leaflet views, including a zoning deep-dive comparing demolished and current stock side by side.
Scale
69,128 buildings · 6 survival models
Role
Pipeline, survival modeling, dashboard
Stack
Python · Lifelines · PyTorch (PyCox) · scikit-survival · Chart.js · Leaflet
Output
Model comparison feeding a paper in preparation
